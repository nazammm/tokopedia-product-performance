# Data

`raw/prdect_id.csv` is the PRDECT-ID dataset, unchanged except for the file name.

- Source: https://github.com/rhiosutoyo/PRDECT-ID-Indonesian-Product-Reviews-Dataset
  (also on Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jocelyndumlao/prdect-id-indonesian-emotion-classification)
- License: MIT, see `raw/LICENSE-PRDECT-ID`
- Paper: Sutoyo, R., Achmad, S., Chowanda, A., Andangsari, E. W., & Isa, S. M. (2022).
  PRDECT-ID: Indonesian product reviews dataset for emotions classification tasks.
  *Data in Brief*, 44, 108554.

5,400 rows, one per review, across 29 Tokopedia categories. Columns: Category, Product Name,
Location (seller city), Price, Overall Rating, Number Sold, Total Review, Customer Rating,
Customer Review, Sentiment, Emotion.
